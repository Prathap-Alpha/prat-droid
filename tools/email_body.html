Prat-Droid v1.0.4 is ready. Install it on your S25 Ultra here:

Download Prat-Droid.apk

To install: tap the link on the phone → it downloads → tap Open → allow install from this source (and if it's blocked, turn off Settings → Security & privacy → Auto Blocker) → Install. Turn the volume up for the splash.

What's new since the first build:

  • Targets Android 15 — installs cleanly past Play Protect.
  • Flashlight — one-tap torch.
  • Notes — quick scratchpad, saved on the device.
  • Device — battery, storage, model, Android version.
  • Screen recording — captures to a video in your gallery (Movies/Prat-Droid).
  • Quick Send — pick a contact, message opens in WhatsApp ready to send.
  • Plus reminders, day plan, calendar, and the Thusa meeting link.

Automatic updates (optional): install Obtainium, add app, paste https://github.com/Prathap-Alpha/prat-droid — every future version installs with one tap.
