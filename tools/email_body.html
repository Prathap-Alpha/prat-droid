Prat-Droid v1.0.6 — now with the Android Auto interface for the Raptor. Install link:

Download Prat-Droid.apk

On the phone: tap the link → Open → allow install from this source (if blocked, Settings → Security & privacy → Auto Blocker = off) → Install.

To get it on the Raptor's screen:

  1. Open the Android Auto settings on the phone.
  2. Scroll to Version and tap it ~10 times — Developer settings unlocks.
  3. Turn on Unknown sources.
  4. Connect the phone to the Raptor by USB — Prat-Droid appears in the Android Auto app launcher. Opening it shows your photo + "Welcome, Prat" → Enter → Quick Message.

Note: Android Auto only allows its own car-safe templated screens, so this is the photo startup + a quick-message list — not the full phone app on the car screen. The full app (splash, reminders, notes, screen recording, flashlight, device status) stays on the phone.
